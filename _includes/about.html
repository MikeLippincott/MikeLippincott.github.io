About

My scientific vision is to bridge cell biology and computational science to advance fundamental biological knowledge to improve human health outcomes.


I am keen on understanding complex, dynamic cell death mechanisms via innovative, high throughput, high content microscopy and machine learning techniques. I am paving the way to model complex cell processes through temporal and 3D image data. It is with this approach that new interventions can be discovered to improve human health outcomes.
